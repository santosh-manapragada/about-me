04 / WHAT I HAVE LEARNED

Principles that survived technology changes.

01

Fundamentals compound.

Frameworks change. Engineering fundamentals continue to create leverage.

02

Architecture is about trade-offs.

A good architecture is not a diagram. It is a set of deliberate decisions under constraints.

03

Learning is not collecting technologies.

The valuable question is not “What should I learn?” but “What capability do I need next?”

04

Technology changes careers.

Each major technology shift creates new roles, new expectations and new ways of working.

05

Adaptability is a capability.

The ability to learn, apply, reflect and adapt becomes more valuable as change accelerates.

06

Stay close to the technology.

Leadership and architecture become stronger when grounded in real engineering experience.
